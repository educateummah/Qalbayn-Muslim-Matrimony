The process

From registration to introduction

A small number of deliberate steps, designed so every conversation starts with real understanding — not a swipe.

01

Register & interview

You answer detailed, conversation-style questions about your values, expectations, and daily life — not just bio-data.

02

We curate

Our team reviews your answers against our database and selects five to ten genuinely considered matches.

03

Families meet

A one-on-one video session, with parents present, so everyone gets a real sense of each other early on.

04

You take it forward

If both sides agree, we share contact details. From there, it's between the two families.

Our commitment

Done the right way, from the start

Every part of the process is built with sensitivity to what families in our community actually expect.

A halal process, throughout

Every step, from the interview to the family video session, is designed to be respectful and in line with halal principles. There is no unchaperoned messaging or private contact until both families agree.

Your privacy is protected

Your phone number and address stay private and are only shared after both sides, and their families, mutually agree to move forward.

No photos on the platform

We don't ask for or display photos anywhere on the platform. Matching is based on who you are, not a picture — appearance is discussed only once families choose to connect directly.
